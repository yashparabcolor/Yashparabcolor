About me

Mumbai-based colourist working in film and post-production.

My work spans OTT web series, Bollywood films, television, advertising and music videos — shaping the final look of a story, one frame at a time.

Trained in cinematography (BMM, Mithibai College), I build my grades in DaVinci Resolve with a focus on colour science, film stock emulation, and technically accurate HDR/SDR delivery.

Previously at Akruti Productions under senior colourist Ganesh Navgane. Currently freelancing.
